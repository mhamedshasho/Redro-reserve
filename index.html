How to use

A practical guide to managing reservations

Quick start

Follow these steps to set up and run your reservation desk

1. Add your resources

Open Tables & devices and add each table, PlayStation station, room or other reservable item. Disable a resource to stop offering it without deleting its booking history.

2. Create a booking

Choose an active resource, date and start time. Enter an end time or switch to duration. Customer name, phone and notes are optional.

3. Avoid conflicts

The app rejects overlapping bookings for the same resource on the same day. Bookings are same-day only; overnight reservations are not supported.

4. Manage bookings

Use Bookings to search and filter. Edit or extend a booking after checking the schedule. Complete or cancel it when needed; cancelled and completed bookings remain in history.

5. Acknowledge alarms

When a booking reaches its end time, the app shows it in Time monitor and plays an alarm while the app is open and audio is allowed. Acknowledge it to stop repeat alerts.

6. Protect your data

Export a JSON backup regularly. Restore it on another device using Replace or Merge. Replace clears existing local data; Merge keeps existing records and adds or updates records with matching IDs.

Important: local-only app

There is no server, cloud database, login or external API. Each browser/device has its own separate data. No automatic synchronization is performed. Keep backups safe.

Alarm limitations

Keep this page open and allow sound. Browsers may block audio until you interact with the page, and phones may suspend background tabs. Alarms cannot be guaranteed when the app is closed or suspended.
